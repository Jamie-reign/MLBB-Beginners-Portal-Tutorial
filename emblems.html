ITEMS AND EMBLEMS


ITEM FUNCTIONALITY

Items greatly influence a hero’s performance by providing attack, defense, or utility boosts. Building the right items at the right time is essential for survival and success.

COUNTER ITEMS

Counter items help shut down enemy strategies. For example, anti-heal items can stop lifesteal-dependent heroes from dominating fights.

EMBLEM MASTERY

Emblems provide extra stats and bonuses suited to different roles. Choosing the right emblem setup enhances a hero’s efficiency in battle.

FLEXIBLE BUILDS

There’s no perfect fixed build for every game. Adapting your items and emblems based on the enemy’s lineup ensures you always stay effective.
