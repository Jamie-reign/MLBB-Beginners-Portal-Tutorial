GAMEPLAY AND STRATEGY


LANE ROTATION

Proper rotation and map awareness allow teams to secure objectives and pressure enemies. Core and roam roles especially rely on smart rotations to maintain control.

BATTLE SPELLS

Using the right battle spell is crucial. Junglers benefit most from Retribution, while roamers and fragile heroes often use Flicker or Purify to survive.

TEAM FIGHT AND POSITIONING

Good positioning determines the outcome of teamfights. Tanks protect the frontline, while damage dealers stay safe in the backline to deal consistent damage.

ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES

Each role has a unique duty—junglers secure buffs, roamers provide vision, while marksmen and mages focus on damage. Understanding these roles keeps the team balanced and leads to victory.
